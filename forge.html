Who approves what

Client brands (ATP, CTF, PLT) are approved by Dillon. Partner brands (TL, GU, OTX) sit on the same board but route to David. Nothing builds until the approver says yes. Verdicts and notes go back to the Idea Library every hour. BRT stays out of FORGE for now.

The weekly FORGE loop

People feed it first. The Ideas Library and the ad data only fill the gaps. Three human gates sit between an idea and a brief, so nothing moves on its own.

Where it sits in the monthly plan

Today FORGE runs every week. Once the monthly plan starts, the big FORGE run moves to the last week of the month: it builds next month's plan, and once that is approved every brief in the plan gets built at once. The weekly loop stays as a smaller top-up for anything new mid-month.

Human gates, on purpose

  • Seed first. Client ideas (Wednesday) and our own (Friday) go on the board before anything the library or data suggests.
  • Every card shows where it came from: Strategist, Dillon, David, Client, Library or Data.
  • Nothing auto-advances. The Monday call shapes the board, Dillon approves client brands, David approves partner brands.
  • Guard: a board can't go out unless every brand has at least one idea from a person, or is marked "no client input this cycle" so the gap shows.

Friday capture skill

A skill Hannes and Dillon each run with their own agent on Friday while they talk through ideas. It turns the conversation into FORGE cards and labels who said what.

What it asks for, per brand: what's working and should get a new version, what's new (drops, designs, footage), anything from the Wednesday client replies, and any idea worth testing. It pulls last 30 days from Atria before suggesting a hook, so every card starts from what is actually winning.
